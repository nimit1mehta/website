Questions, answered

What data and AI teams ask before they commit.

What is TQ Data Foundation?

One place to govern what your data means, where it lives and how it may be used, then serve it as authoritative context to every agent, application and analyst.

How is this different from a data catalog?

A catalog inventories where data lives and infers context at scale. TQ governs the context your experts approve and binds it to every system, including the catalog. Read the market guide

Do we have to move our data into TQ?

No. Context is bound to the systems where your data already lives, with 200+ prebuilt connectors. See connectors

How do agents consume the context?

Through MCP, APIs and SPARQL, so an agent retrieves governed meaning at query time and cites what it used. See AI governance

Are we locked in?

No. Everything is built on open W3C standards our team helped author, so what you model stays portable.
